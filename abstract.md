Journals require that you **share your code and data** in a replication package at the end of your research project. That can be a challenge, when some parts of your research data are confidential. 

Following some best practices from day 1 can not only **help you prepare** this package later, but also make you **more productive** researchers. Following some best practices before releasing a package can **avoid costly revisions**. 

This document provides guidance on how to ensure the reproducibility and transparency of your research when **data are confidential**. It is not meant to be exhaustive, and it is not meant to be prescriptive. There are many ways to construct a replication package, and even more situations in which confidential data are housed.
